### Agree on the roads and labels
- Three smooth, three bumpy segments per participant
- Prefer continuous segments without junctions, sharp turns, or frequent stops.
- Record a short description explaining the label and identify clear start/end landmarks.


### Agree on phone placement and equipment
- Use the same snug body location and phone orientation every time.
- Participants use own phone, doesn't move in pocket. Face down. 
- Keep setup consistent. 

### Agree on how to ride
- Use a similar comfortable speed, remain seated, and pedal normally.
- Every participant should ride both surface classes and all selected segments.
- Document unavoidable braking, swerving, standing, interruptions, or changes in conditions.

*Easily missed: riding smooth roads slowly and rough roads quickly makes speed a competing explanation for the results. Returning along the same road is another pass—not a new independent location.*
